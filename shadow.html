Governing principle

Utility is relational, not existential.

The system measures whether a state contributes to the immediate order. It does not treat that measurement as a verdict on whether the state deserves to exist. Being, considering, attempting, trying, hiding, deferring, infecting or conferring may be useful now, obstructive now, or outside the order entirely. The latter category is retained as legitimate remainder when its place can be rationally described.

Immediate orderMovement, protection, resolution or delivery that matters now.
